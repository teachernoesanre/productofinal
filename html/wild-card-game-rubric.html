WILD CARD GAME RUBRIC

Rúbrica

CRITERIA

 EXCELLENT (4)

 GOOD (3) IN PROGRESS (2) NEEDS IMPROVEMENT (1)
 1. Production of comparatives and superlatives (ING.6.2.1 – ING.6.2.2)
     Uses comparatives and superlatives accurately, fluently and independently.
 Uses structures correctly with minor mistakes that do not affect meaning.
 Needs support and makes frequent errors when forming sentences. Cannot produce comparatives or superlatives without constant help.
 2. Oral interaction during the game (ING.6.3.1 – ING.6.3.2)
   Participates actively, interacts naturally and respects turns and rules.
 Participates appropriately with some hesitation or occasional support.   Irregular participation and needs frequent teacher support.
 Minimal or no meaningful oral interaction.
 3. Language accuracy (vocabulary + grammar) (ING.6.2.3)
     Correct and accurate use of vocabulary and grammar structures.
 Minor mistakes that do not interfere with communication. Frequent errors that sometimes affect understanding. Communication is unclear due to persistent errors.
 4. Understanding of game instructions (ING.6.1.2)
     Fully understands and follows instructions autonomously.
 Understands instructions with occasional help. Needs frequent guidance to follow the game. Does not understand instructions without constant help.
 5. Attitude and cooperation (ING.6.3.1 – ING.6.6.1)
     Very positive attitude, cooperative and respectful at all times.
 Good attitude with minor reminders needed. Passive or irregular participation in group work. Disruptive or non-cooperative behaviour.
